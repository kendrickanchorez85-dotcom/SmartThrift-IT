OUR STORY

SmartThrift IT was made by four people from BSIT1-4. They want to create a website that sells pre-loved clothes while sellers and customers dont have to bother going out. Their idea comes to live and as a result , they created this website.

WHY PRE-LOVED?

- AFFORDABLE: Designer quality at fraction of price

- TIMELESS: Styles that never go out of fashion
